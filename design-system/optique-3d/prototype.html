Design system

The accent only works on dark.

Every pair below was computed, not estimated. Electric blue is legible on ink and fails on cream — which is why the brand reserves it for the technology, and why light surfaces use a darker same-hue variant instead.

--accent #6C8CFF 6.34:1 on ink · PASS
#6C8CFF on cream 2.80:1 · FAIL
--accent-light #335FFF 4.53:1 on cream · PASS
--border-strong #8A8A83 3.16:1 on cream · PASS
--hairline #D9D9D4 1.29:1 on cream · decorative only
--cream #F6F4EF 17.72:1 on ink · AAA

A white label on a blue button reads at only 3.07:1, so the blue button carries a black label at 6.34:1 — visible on every CTA above. On cream, the primary button is ink, not blue.
